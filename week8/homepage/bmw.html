BMW G30 520d

A benchmark for reliability and comfort in the premium sedan segment.

Technical Specifications

Engine 2.0L Diesel B47
Power 190 HP
Fuel Consumption ~5.0 L/100km
Transmission 8-speed Automatic

Overview

The BMW G30 520d combines a proven diesel engine with excellent driving dynamics, making it one of the most balanced choices in its class. The B47 engine has a solid reputation for durability when properly maintained.

Interior quality, ride comfort, and technology all remain competitive years after release, which is why the G30 holds its value well on the used market.

Pros & Cons

  • Reliable and efficient engine
  • Comfortable, well-balanced ride
  • Strong resale value
  • Higher maintenance costs than non-premium brands
  • Timing chain issues on early production years

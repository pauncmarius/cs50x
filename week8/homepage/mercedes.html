Mercedes W213

Where comfort, prestige, and long-distance refinement meet.

Technical Specifications

Engine 2.0L Diesel OM654
Power 194 HP
Fuel Consumption ~5.1 L/100km
Transmission 9-speed 9G-Tronic

Overview

The Mercedes W213 E-Class delivers a level of comfort and refinement that few competitors can match, particularly on long highway drives. Its OM654 engine is known for smooth power delivery and reasonable efficiency.

The cabin feels genuinely upscale, with soft-touch materials and a calm, quiet driving experience that reinforces the car's prestige image.

Pros & Cons

  • Outstanding ride comfort
  • Premium, upscale interior
  • Strong prestige and resale image
  • Some electronic components can be costly to repair
  • Higher purchase price than comparable BMW/Audi trims

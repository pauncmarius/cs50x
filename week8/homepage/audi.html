Audi A5 B9

Sleek design meets refined engineering in one of Audi's most elegant sedans.

Technical Specifications

Engine 2.0L Diesel EA288 evo
Power 190 HP
Fuel Consumption ~5.2 L/100km
Transmission 7-speed S tronic

Overview

The Audi A5 B9 stands out with its striking design and premium cabin materials. The available quattro all-wheel drive adds confidence in all weather conditions, making it a strong choice for year-round driving.

Its EA288 evo engine offers a good balance between performance and efficiency, and the overall build quality reflects Audi's attention to detail.

Pros & Cons

  • Elegant, timeless design
  • Excellent interior fit and finish
  • Available quattro all-wheel drive
  • Repairs can be costly outside warranty
  • Firmer ride compared to competitors
